Welcome to Pioneer at a Glance

A simple place to plan your ministry and see your progress without digging through notes or spreadsheets.

  • Track daily hours and your 50-hour monthly goal.
  • See weekly and September–August service-year progress.
  • Schedule public witnessing and other assignments.
  • Keep notes for studies, return visits, and experiences.
  • Your data stays on your device unless you export a backup.
